# Creative reference study

Reviewed 30 October 2026 through Firecrawl. These are references for direction, not sources of NEUAURA capabilities. All NEUAURA product geometry, vector artwork and copy in this project are original. No competitor media was downloaded or reused.

- [WearSynapse](https://www.wearsynapse.com/) and [product](https://www.wearsynapse.com/product): minimal product-first storytelling, contextual use cases and a clear hardware/software narrative. The founder's specific transformation video was not attached; the implemented mechanical sequence is original.
- [NeoSapien](https://neosapien.ai/): explain the usefulness of conversation context through concrete tasks, rather than presenting AI as an abstract feature. Vendor-specific capabilities are not attributed to NEUAURA.
- [Plaud NotePin](https://in.plaud.ai/products/plaud-notepin): multiple wearing attachments around compact hardware. NEUAURA uses its own provisional industrial design and geometry.
- [Nothing](https://us.nothing.tech/about): restrained industrial expression and transparent material language. Its branding, artwork and layouts are not reproduced.
- [Ray-Ban Meta](https://india.ray-ban.com/discover/discover-rayaban.html): balance familiar eyewear proportions with subtle technology placement; no product dimensions or specifications were borrowed.
- [Oura finishes](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/21255327138579-Oura-Ring-Finishes): deliberate material treatment and controlled finish presentation. No durability/coating claims are made for NEUAURA.
- [Apple titanium manufacturing](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/11/mapping-the-future-with-3d-printed-titanium-apple-watch-cases/): manufacturing-led detail, precise highlights and material-led storytelling; no manufacturing readiness is implied for this project.
- [B2B wearables market context](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/b2b-enterprise-and-industrial-wearables-market): contextual understanding of enterprise applications. No market numbers, ROI, adoption rates or customer outcomes are used on the site.
- [AI4Bharat ASSIST / Indian Sign Language research](https://sign-language.ai4bharat.org/): external context for accessible communication. The site links to this research and explicitly distinguishes it from NEUAURA development, validated capabilities and partnerships.
