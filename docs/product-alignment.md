# Product alignment

Product source: NEUAURA_B2B_Wearable_Project_Overview.docx, supplied by Ramesh Babu from Downloads and read in full for this revision. The document supplies product context; the founder’s message directs the website’s emphasis.

The main narrative is customer interactions → consented capture → background processing and extraction → connected business context → owner workspace → planned BMS/CRM connectivity. Meeting approvals and task confirmation are supporting controls, rather than the product proposition.

## Architecture mapping

- Overview sections 1, 3 and 7: capture, processing, context, application and integration layers are shown in the six-layer interactive architecture. The processing layer includes ingestion, transcription, cleanup, summarisation, entity extraction and structured output.
- Sections 2 and 5: customer needs, objections, relationship continuity and owner visibility lead the hero, problem section and owner briefing.
- Sections 4 and 6: field sales and business development remain initial pilot workflows. Account relationships and service/operations are adjacent applications of the common architecture. The site does not claim proven fit for every business or completed advanced team analytics.
- Sections 6 and 7: basic export is demonstrated with local fictional JSON. APIs, BMS/CRM connectivity, configurable workflows and advanced analytics remain later scope.
- Sections 7, 8 and 13: source-linked correction, consent, visible capture controls, deletion and consequential-action confirmation remain in the record and trust flows. Background processing does not mean secret recording or autonomous decisions.
- Sections 10 and 12: approximately 2–5 organisations, one repeatable workflow, baseline measurements and a six-phase development roadmap remain.
- Sections 1, 6 and 12: software validation can use phones, computers and compatible devices. The pendant and screenless wristband remain future interfaces to the intelligence layer, sharing one core; no hardware specifications or availability are invented.

## Demonstration boundaries

The architecture simulation and qualitative owner briefing use three fictional records: a customer visit, a partnership discussion and a service visit. They perform no audio capture, AI inference, remote processing or BMS transmission. The architecture payload labels its record as draft until reviewed and its destination as preview only. Pilot enquiries remain honest unsent drafts unless a real delivery endpoint is configured.

No new product capabilities are inferred from the overview’s optional technology suggestions. The existing React/Vite stack is retained. The original supplied founder note and direct email remain unchanged.
